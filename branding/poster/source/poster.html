01PROBLEM

  • Volunteers run on spreadsheets, forms and chat groups
  • Hard to match the right volunteer to the right activity
  • Attendance, hours and contributions are hard to prove
  • No data on participation, retention or skills shortages
  • Existing tools serve one organisation, not many NGOs
One platform that takes volunteers from first sign-up to proven impact.

02APPROACH TO SOLVE

One centralised multi-NGO web platform for every role.

  • AI recommends, humans decide: Keyword, Semantic and Hybrid scoring on skills, availability, location, experience and interests
  • Design-first: Figma wireframes drive the build; privacy by design (PDPA 2010)
Research question: how much do semantic and hybrid matching improve accuracy over keyword matching?
